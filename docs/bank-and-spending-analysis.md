# Bank imports and spending history

ABC debit exports use compact YYYYMMDD dates, signed transaction amount, separate balance, and wrapped merchant cells. CITIC exports use separate RMB credit/debit columns; right edges identify the numeric columns. Known certificate-only pages are excluded. Unknown or incomplete transaction pages block import; a zero-row extraction now shows a failure instead of a success.

Private validation (not checked in): ABC 11 pages, 449 nonzero transactions, 4 zero rows excluded; CITIC 10 pages, 281 transactions, final certificate page excluded. Date, amount, and direction independently matched with PyMuPDF for every nonzero row. Synthetic layout fixtures cover missing headers and ambiguous columns. No personal statements, text dumps, names, account numbers, or transactions are part of the repository changes.

History covers up to 365 calendar days ending at the latest past record, including inactive days; a coverage span does not prove every file is complete. Gross expense, identified refund, net expense and routine expense are distinct. Income, repayment and neutral/internal transfers are not consumption. Routine spending excludes rent/mortgage and transactions of at least CNY1000. Linked routine refunds reduce the routine baseline. Unknown refunds are not inferred from a bank merchant name.

Charts: monthly net-spending bars and daily net spending with a trailing seven-day mean. Monthly table includes partial-month coverage days, gross, refunds, net and daily net. Category rules, unclassified counts, pending rows and potential cross-source duplicates are disclosed. Same date/amount plus platform merchant on bank-side rows only flags candidates; the user confirms which bank-side rows to exclude, with restore. Original rows remain available. Confirmed exclusions also affect forecasts and budget estimation.

Chat uses history for target comparisons, 30-day delta, observed seven-day-window target frequency and forecast scenarios. Daily targets default to routine spending; explicit all-cost targets subtract identified fixed costs for the daily forecast component, and explain missing future fixed plans. Speculation does not change the budget. Explicit adoption changes it and preserves the manual mode. Follow-up budgets and Chinese numerals are supported. Historical frequency does not guarantee affordability or adherence. This is deterministic browser-local analysis, not a model API or a bank connection.
